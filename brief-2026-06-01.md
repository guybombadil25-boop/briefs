# Investment Brief — 2026-06-01

## AI Infrastructure
- **Majestic Labs raised a $100M Series A** for a memory-pooled AI server design that claims up to 100 TB of DRAM per accelerator. If validated, this attacks the HBM bottleneck and could shift inference economics away from pure GPU rack scaling.
- **Bit Digital originated a $100M loan tied to WhiteFiber’s North Carolina HPC campus**, using an Ethereum-denominated credit facility to finance AI compute infrastructure. Crypto balance sheets are increasingly being repurposed into AI data-center credit.
- **AI data-center growth is hitting power constraints**, with fresh reporting highlighting grid bottlenecks and rising build costs. The constraint is becoming less “can we buy GPUs?” and more “can we secure powered sites?”

## Robotics
- **Zhongke Diwuji closed a Series A worth several hundred million yuan** for embodied AI models for robotics. China’s physical-AI stack is attracting state-backed capital and pushing toward reusable “robot brain” models rather than one-off hardware.
- **Foundation Robotics Labs is reportedly targeting military humanoid deployments within 12–18 months**, with Phantom robots already tested for logistics-style use cases. Defense may become an early high-ASP market for humanoids before general factory labor scales.
- **Neura Robotics is reportedly seeking up to €1B for humanoid production**, with follow-on reports suggesting large strategic/backer interest. Not confirmed as fully closed, but it signals Europe’s humanoid race is moving from demos to capacity financing.

## Energy
- **Thea Energy raised a $100M Series B** to build scalable stellarator fusion power plants. Fusion capital is increasingly backing differentiated architectures as AI-driven power demand strengthens the strategic value of firm clean energy.
- **Energy Vault closed its acquisition of an 850 MW Japanese battery-storage portfolio from BayWa r.e.** The deal gives it an immediate Japan platform and reinforces that grid-scale storage is becoming a developer/operator consolidation market.
- **The IEA said India’s energy investment is set to reach $170B in 2026**, led by solar, grid, and refining. India remains one of the biggest demand-side laboratories for grid expansion, renewables, and industrial electrification.

## Crypto
- **U.S. crypto groups escalated pressure for a Senate vote after the CLARITY Act advanced 15–9 in committee.** Market-structure legislation is moving from concept to legislative trench warfare, with SEC/CFTC jurisdiction and DeFi carveouts the key swing factors.
- **The CLARITY Act’s stablecoin compromise reportedly bans interest-bearing payment stablecoins while allowing certain rewards.** That would reshape product design for stablecoin issuers, exchanges, and DeFi front ends if it survives the full process.
- **Fasset raised a $51M Series B to expand stablecoin-enabled financial infrastructure in emerging markets.** The round clears the >$50M signal threshold and shows institutional capital still backing stablecoin distribution rails despite regulatory uncertainty.

## Emerging Tech
- **Inceptive raised $100M for AI-designed RNA therapeutics.** The round is a high-signal bet that generative biology can move beyond discovery tooling into programmable medicines with platform economics.
- **The U.S. government reportedly moved toward ~$2B of CHIPS Act support for nine quantum companies via minority equity stakes.** If finalized, it would be the strongest federal demand signal yet for domestic quantum hardware and infrastructure.
- **QTUM surpassed $5B in assets as quantum funding headlines accelerated.** Public-market appetite for quantum exposure is rising before clear commercial revenue, making government milestones and error-correction proof points especially thesis-relevant.

## Watch List
- **Neura Robotics financing**: confirm whether the reported €1B round closes and who leads it; strategic investors would validate humanoid manufacturing scale-up.
- **CLARITY Act floor path**: watch whether DeFi language survives Senate negotiation or gets narrowed under banking-system risk concerns.
- **AI data-center credit structures**: monitor more crypto/HPC financing hybrids after the Bit Digital–WhiteFiber loan; it could become a repeatable funding model for powered compute sites.
