# Investment Brief — 2026-05-28

## AI Infrastructure
- **Modal Labs raised a $355M Series C at a $4.65B valuation** for serverless AI cloud infrastructure. The round signals that investors are still backing developer-facing compute layers where usage abstraction, not raw GPU ownership, is the wedge.
- **Micron briefly crossed $1T in market value as AI memory demand accelerated.** HBM and advanced memory are becoming a core AI bottleneck, widening the infrastructure thesis beyond GPUs and fabs.
- **AI power constraints stayed front-and-center: Bloom Energy’s Nebius deal points to 250MW of dedicated fuel-cell capacity for data centers.** On-site power is moving from a nice-to-have to a procurement requirement for AI clusters.
- **Advanced packaging remains a bottleneck, with reports that TSMC is racing to double capacity by year-end.** If CoWoS-style packaging, not compute die supply, gates accelerator output, value accrues to packaging capacity and HBM integration.

## Robotics
- **Humanoid partnered with Bosch to scale production of its HMND industrial robots.** A manufacturing partner with Bosch’s footprint moves humanoids from demo cadence toward repeatable factory deployment.
- **China launched a humanoid robot lifecycle ID platform, with reports of 28,000+ robots already registered.** Formal tracking infrastructure suggests Beijing is preparing for scaled deployment, safety monitoring, and industrial policy enforcement.
- **Stord raised $250M and is building an AI robotics lab for fulfillment automation.** Logistics players are increasingly combining warehouse software, physical automation, and fulfillment networks to compete with Amazon-like vertical integration.

## Energy
- **Ford Energy and EDF reportedly signed a 20GWh battery storage deal.** Large storage offtake deals are becoming a practical grid-resilience and electrification lever, not just a renewable attachment.
- **Adani Green operationalized a 3.37GWh battery system at Khavda.** Utility-scale storage is moving into multi-GWh deployment, reinforcing the thesis that dispatchability is the scarce layer in renewables.
- **Dow and X-energy’s Texas advanced nuclear project received an NRC environmental assessment with a finding of no significant impact.** Faster environmental clearance de-risks industrial heat and SMR timelines for power-hungry manufacturing and AI loads.
- **Oklo’s recent NRC progress and AI-power positioning kept nuclear in the data-center conversation.** Regulatory milestones, not just PPAs, remain the key gating factor for advanced nuclear investability.

## Crypto
- **Banca Sella became the first Italian bank cleared to offer crypto-asset services under MiCA.** Bank-led custody and transfer services are a stronger institutional adoption signal than offshore exchange growth.
- **The SEC’s tokenized-stock “innovation exemption” debate continued, with scope reportedly narrowed around synthetic tokens.** The direction still favors regulated tokenization, but Wall Street pushback is defining what products can actually launch.
- **Fasset’s $51M Series B for stablecoin-powered banking stayed relevant as emerging-market payments infrastructure gains traction.** Stablecoins are increasingly being funded as banking rails, not just crypto trading collateral.
- **Multiple Ethereum infrastructure projects, including Everclear, ZERO Network, and Syndicate Labs, announced wind-downs around the same window.** The L2/modular infra stack is consolidating; durable revenue and distribution matter more than protocol count.

## Emerging Tech
- **IBM committed more than $10B over five years toward a large-scale fault-tolerant quantum computer by 2029.** The quantum thesis is shifting from lab milestones to industrial-capex roadmaps with public-company balance sheets.
- **Quantinuum filed for an IPO targeting roughly a $12B+ valuation and up to ~$1B raised.** A successful listing would create a new public-market benchmark for quantum hardware and software stacks.
- **The U.S. Commerce Department’s ~$2B quantum incentive package across nine companies drew legal and market scrutiny.** Government equity-linked support could accelerate domestic quantum supply chains, but the structure may face policy challenges.
- **Gene Solutions’ SPOT-MAS 10 multi-cancer screening test received FDA Breakthrough Device Designation.** Liquid biopsy remains one of the highest-upside diagnostics categories if regulatory acceleration translates into coverage and adoption.

## Watch List
- **SpaceX IPO spillover:** Reuters reported U.S. space stocks rising on SpaceX IPO hype; watch whether the offering reprices launch, satellite, and space-infrastructure comps.
- **Data-center backlash:** Local and state-level moratorium efforts around AI data centers are worth tracking as a potential constraint on site selection and power procurement.
- **EU MiCA implementation:** Banca Sella’s clearance may be the first of a broader wave of bank crypto custody launches under the lighter credit-institution notification path.
