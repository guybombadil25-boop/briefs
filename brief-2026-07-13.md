# Investment Brief — 2026-07-13

## AI Infrastructure
- **TeraWulf arranged a $3.5B leveraged loan for AI data-center buildout.** Debt markets are now underwriting AI infrastructure at utility-scale, but higher leverage makes power access and utilization rates the key risk variables.
- **Meta will reportedly put an in-house AI chip into production in September.** Hyperscalers are still pushing custom silicon to reduce dependence on Nvidia and bend inference-cost curves.
- **U.S. data-center opposition is becoming material, with reports of $130B+ in blocked or delayed projects this year.** Local permitting, water, and grid constraints are now thesis-level bottlenecks for AI capacity growth.
- **Anthropic is reportedly in early talks with Samsung for a custom AI chip.** Frontier labs moving toward proprietary silicon would shift value from pure GPU resale toward foundry, HBM, and chip-design ecosystems.

## Robotics
- **AI² Robotics raised $735M at a reported $3B valuation for wheeled humanoid robots.** The round is a major vote for embodied-AI platforms, especially architectures that trade full bipedal complexity for near-term factory economics.
- **Ant Group led a roughly $74M round in Chinese humanoid startup Zeroth.** Alibaba-linked capital deepens China’s humanoid supply-chain race and could compress hardware costs faster than U.S. incumbents expect.
- **Automate 2026 set an attendance record, with A3 citing surging demand for robotics, AI, and automation.** The signal is less about one product and more about buyer urgency as labor scarcity and reshoring pull automation into mainstream capex.

## Energy
- **Aalo Atomics became the fourth advanced U.S. reactor to reach criticality under the DOE push, surpassing the White House target.** Advanced nuclear is moving from paper designs to test reactors, but the investable question remains repeatable licensing and commercial deployment speed.
- **The IAEA and World Bank announced collaboration to advance nuclear energy for development.** Multilateral financing support could open nuclear buildouts in countries where grid growth and AI/industrial power demand are colliding.
- **Tech giants have now reportedly pledged more than 10 GW of new nuclear power for AI.** The AI power thesis is shifting from spot power procurement to long-duration, firm-power contracting with nuclear as a strategic input.

## Crypto
- **The SEC’s 2026 agenda reportedly puts crypto rulemaking on the July calendar, including safe harbors, custody, and ATS/trading venue rules.** If implemented, this could move U.S. crypto from enforcement-first ambiguity toward regulated infrastructure buildout.
- **The CLARITY Act missed its July 4 target, with an August 7 deadline now in focus.** Market-structure legislation remains the central U.S. policy catalyst; slippage raises timing risk but not necessarily thesis failure.
- **The EU is reportedly reopening MiCA stablecoin reserve rules after concerns that the framework advantaged Circle.** Stablecoin regulation is entering a second-order phase where reserve design and issuer competition matter as much as basic legality.

## Emerging Tech
- **Oratomic raised $300M Series A to build a fault-tolerant quantum computer targeting ~20,000 qubits.** The size of the round shows investors are willing to fund full-stack quantum architecture bets before commercial utility is proven.
- **Chips JU and Pasqal launched Q-PLANET, a €50M pilot line to industrialize neutral-atom quantum chips.** Europe is moving quantum from lab systems toward manufacturable hardware, a key bottleneck for scaling.
- **Inceptive reportedly raised $100M for AI-designed RNA medicines.** AI-native biotech funding is concentrating around platforms that can turn model outputs into programmable therapeutic modalities.
- **Venus Aerospace closed a $91M Series B for propulsive technology.** Space and hypersonics capital is flowing into dual-use systems where defense demand can subsidize commercial frontier-tech roadmaps.

## Watch List
- **SEC “Regulation Crypto” details:** Track whether July proposals create real exemptions/safe harbors or simply repackage registration requirements.
- **Advanced nuclear criticality-to-contract conversion:** Watch whether Aalo, Valar, Antares, and peers turn test milestones into paid deployments for data centers or defense.
- **AI data-center permitting backlash:** Follow whether blocked projects force a pivot toward smaller edge sites, behind-the-meter power, or acquisitions of generation assets.
