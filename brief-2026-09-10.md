# Investment Brief — 2026-09-10

## AI Infrastructure
- **[Amazon agreed to buy up to $60B of Qualcomm AI data-center chips](https://www.reuters.com/technology/qualcomm-amazon-develop-custom-chips-ai-data-centers-2026-09-08/)**, with purchase-linked warrants worth roughly $4B. The hyperscaler-scale commitment validates a credible Nvidia inference challenger.
- **[Ayar Labs added $150M to its Series E](https://ayarlabs.com/news/ayar-labs-expands-2026-funding-to-650-million/)**, taking 2026 primary funding to $650M for co-packaged optical links. Capital is concentrating on interconnects as bandwidth and power become cluster bottlenecks.
- **[NEXTDC sought A$1.1B ($796M) in convertible financing](https://www.thestar.com.my/tech/tech-news/2026/09/09/australian-data-centre-operator-nextdc-to-raise-795-million-for-ai-infrastructure)**, its third raise in four months. AI data-center growth is turning operators into persistently capital-hungry infrastructure vehicles.

## Robotics
- **[XPeng commissioned its IRON humanoid production line](https://www.xpeng.com/pressroom/news/01a080371029a057bc8e8a02a2c6012b)** and reaffirmed mass production by year-end, with deliveries targeted for 2027. Humanoids are moving from prototypes to auto-grade manufacturing.
- **[Agility Robotics’ S-4 disclosed $1.8M of 2025 revenue and a $140M operating loss](https://www.therobotreport.com/agility-robotics-reports-18m-revenue-ahead-of-humanoid-spac/)** despite Digit deployments at nine sites. The first detailed humanoid public-market economics expose the gap between pilots and scaled sales.

## Energy
- **[DOE closed a loan of up to $1.9B to restart Iowa’s 615-MW Duane Arnold nuclear plant](https://www.energy.gov/articles/energy-department-closes-19-billion-loan-restart-duane-arnold-nuclear-plant)** by 2029. Federal financing makes reactor restarts a credible near-term source for AI-driven load.
- **[Google committed €13B to Finnish AI and energy infrastructure](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/infrastructure-and-cloud/global-network/google-ai-commitment-to-finland/)** alongside a 22-year nuclear PPA covering up to half of Fortum’s Loviisa output. Hyperscalers are underwriting reactor life extensions for firm power.
- **[The U.S. Navy chose Indiana’s Crane base for its first shore-based advanced microreactor](https://www.war.gov/News/Releases/Release/Article/4593065/department-of-war-announces-navys-first-advanced-nuclear-microreactor-deploymen/)**, targeting installation by September 2028. Success could create a bankable reference case for critical sites.

## Crypto
- **[Tether and Fasanara launched a $400M private-credit fund](https://tether.io/news/tether-and-fasanara-capital-launch-400-million-private-credit-fund-to-expand-stablecoin-enabled-real-economy-lending/)** targeting up to $3B, with USDT settlement. Stablecoins are expanding from payments into institutional credit.
- **[U.S. Bank executed a live cross-border payment using its USBDC stablecoin on Stellar](https://ir.usbank.com/news-events/news/news-details/2026/U-S--Bank-Launches-USBDC-Stablecoin/default.aspx)**. A top-five U.S. commercial bank issuing on a public chain advances bank-controlled stablecoin rails.
- **[Canary’s Staked TRX ETF began trading on Cboe](https://cryptobriefing.com/canary-capital-staked-tron-etf-cboe-listing/)**, combining spot exposure with staking rewards. It broadens regulated U.S. access while normalizing yield-bearing crypto wrappers.

- **[Encoded Therapeutics raised a $275M Series F](https://www.financialcontent.com/article/bizwire-2026-9-9-encoded-therapeutics-raises-275-million-in-series-f-financing-to-advance-etx101-through-pivotal-development-and-accelerate-its-precision-genetic-medicines-neurology-pipeline)** for pivotal development of its Dravet syndrome gene therapy and internal GMP scale-up. Late-stage capital plus owned manufacturing signals confidence in precision genetic medicines.
- **[Quantinuum finalized $100M in CHIPS Act funding](https://thequantuminsider.com/2026/09/08/quantinuum-finalizes-100m-us-government-award-quantum-manufacturing/)** for U.S. quantum-semiconductor R&D and manufacturing. Quantum competition is shifting from lab performance toward supply-chain scale.
