# Investment Brief — 2026-09-25

## AI Infrastructure
- **[Crusoe signed a $65M annual inference agreement with Thinking Machines Lab](https://www.crusoe.ai/resources/newsroom/crusoe-thinking-machines-lab-inference-partnership)** for open-model workloads on dedicated Nvidia B200 systems. The contract pushes Crusoe’s managed-inference business above $100M in contracted annual revenue, validating neocloud demand beyond model training.
- **[Microsoft pledged more than $10B for Gulf cloud and AI infrastructure through 2030](https://www.mobileeurope.co.uk/microsoft-middle-east-ai/),** plus over $400M for regional connectivity. The commitment expands sovereign AI capacity in Kuwait, Qatar, Saudi Arabia, and the UAE despite elevated geopolitical and execution risk.

## Robotics
- **[AGIBOT and Chimelong deployed more than 300 robots at a Zhuhai theme park](https://www.roboticstomorrow.com/news/2026/09/24/agibot-and-chimelong-launch-large-scale-embodied-ai-theme-park-with-more-than-300-robots/27148/)** across entertainment, education, retail, visitor services, and hotel operations. A multi-role fleet in a high-traffic venue is a meaningful test of reliability and unit economics outside controlled factories.
- **[ANYbotics launched automated door access for ANYmal inspection fleets](https://www.therobotreport.com/anybotics-opens-the-door-for-inspections-with-anymal-robots/)** with dormakaba and LEGIC. Letting robots traverse secured facilities without human escorts removes a practical barrier to truly site-wide industrial inspection.

## Energy
- **[The U.S. Energy Department selected 31 grid projects for $1.9B of funding](https://www.energy.gov/articles/energy-department-announces-speed-power-investments-across-26-states-lower-electricity),** targeting 23 GW of additional capacity across 26 states. Reconductoring and grid-enhancing technologies could unlock power faster than building new transmission—critical for AI-load growth.
- **[Nigeria commercially launched a $300M distributed-renewable-energy fund](https://www.msn.com/en-us/news/other/nigeria-launches-300-million-clean-energy-fund-to-scale-off-grid-power-access/ar-AA2cHtBg).** Moving from structuring to capital deployment creates a scalable financing vehicle for off-grid power and private investment under Mission 300.

## Crypto
- **[The CFTC updated its guidance to permit tokenized forms of eligible customer investments and blockchain-based recordkeeping](https://www.cftc.gov/PressRoom/PressReleases/9303-26).** Regulatory equivalence between tokenized and traditional assets lowers a key adoption barrier for U.S. derivatives firms.
- **[Brazil’s central bank imposed reporting on institutional transfers of $10,000 or more involving self-custody wallets](https://www.tftc.io/brazil-self-custody-reporting-resolution-588-589-bcb),** effective October 1. The rule preserves self-custody but extends AML visibility to its institutional on- and off-ramps, a model other jurisdictions may copy.

## Emerging Tech
- **[ADARx raised $446.3M in an upsized U.S. IPO](https://www.reuters.com/legal/litigation/adarx-pharmaceuticals-raises-4463-million-upsized-us-ipo-2026-09-25/)** at $17 per share. The first U.S. RNAi biotech listing since 2024 is a strong reopening signal for capital-intensive platform companies.
- **[Eli Lilly agreed to pay InnoCare up to $3.35B](https://finance.yahoo.com/healthcare/articles/eli-lilly-innocare-sign-drug-171917070.html),** including $100M upfront/near-term, for discovery work across as many as five targets. Another large cross-border deal reinforces China’s growing role as a source of globally licensed drug innovation.
- **[Microsoft will give DARPA hands-on access to a Majorana 2 quantum system at a new Maryland center](https://www.reuters.com/business/microsoft-give-darpa-hands-on-access-to-quantum-system-new-maryland-site-2026-09-22/).** Independent government testing is a consequential credibility gate for Microsoft’s topological-qubit claims and quantum roadmap.

- **Bitget breach:** Early estimates of assets drained range from roughly $183M to $350M; watch the verified loss, reserve coverage, and evidence of any private-key compromise.
