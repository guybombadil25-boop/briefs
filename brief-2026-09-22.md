# Investment Brief — 2026-09-22

## AI Infrastructure
- **[Alibaba launched its Zhenwu V900 accelerator and targeted more than 20 GW of global data-center capacity by 2032](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/22/alibaba-ai-alibabacloud-zhenwu-v900-.html).** The chip reportedly triples its predecessor’s performance, signaling a vertically integrated Chinese challenge to Nvidia-led compute stacks.
- **[Verda raised a $189M Series B at a valuation above $1B](https://verda.com/blog/verda-raises-189m)** to expand GPU capacity, data centers, and inference services. The oversubscribed round takes total funding above $450M and shows capital still rewarding independent full-stack AI clouds.

## Robotics
- **[Faraday Future launched nine AI-powered robots and four industry solutions](https://www.morningstar.com/news/business-wire/20260919657129/faraday-future-launches-four-industry-productivity-solutions-nine-new-eai-devices-at-its-919-event-building-its-one-brain-multi-form-multi-capability-ff-eai-robot-world-20-all-new-futurist-now-on-sale-and-master-mini-starts-at-9990)** spanning humanoid and quadruped form factors, with products starting at $9,990. Broad pricing and immediate sales turn embodied AI from demos toward a commercial portfolio, though customer uptake remains unproven.
- **[LS Electric put two Boston Dynamics Spot robots into regular operation at its Busan transformer plant](https://www.koreatimes.co.kr/business/companies/20260922/ls-electric-deploys-robotic-dogs-to-drive-smart-factory-automation-in-busan).** Monitoring equipment, quality, and safety in live production is a tangible industrial-automation deployment rather than another pilot-stage showcase.

## Energy
- **[Samsung C&T committed up to $100M to Kairos Power’s Hermes 2 demonstration reactor](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/21/kairos-power-gets-up-to-100m-from-samsung-group-to-build-nuclear-reactor-for-google/)** through $70M of equity plus engineering services, subject to approvals. Pairing reactor capital with an experienced nuclear builder lowers execution risk on Kairos’s path to Google-backed commercial deployment.
- **[Turkey’s Akkuyu 1 advanced to its next pre-commissioning stage](https://www.world-nuclear-news.org/articles/akkuyu-1-set-for-next-stage-of-pre-commissioning-activities)** as it targets first power in 2026. Progress at the country’s first nuclear unit matters for a four-reactor, 4.8 GW buildout and for Rosatom’s build-own-operate export model.

## Crypto
- **[Ondo enabled institutions to convert stocks and ETFs directly into tokenized equivalents](https://ondo.finance/blog/convert-shares-to-tokenized-stocks)** through Alpaca on Ethereum and BNB Chain. In-kind minting and redemption removes a cash leg, making tokenized securities more usable as institutional market infrastructure.
- **[Kakao Pay and Kakao Bank signed an MoU with Fireblocks](https://technode.global/2026/09/22/kakao-pay-kakao-bank-fireblocks-digital-asset-infrastructure-korea/)** to explore stablecoins and secure digital-asset infrastructure in South Korea. The combination of mass-market distribution, banking rails, and institutional custody could accelerate adoption if Korea’s rules solidify.

## Emerging Tech
- **[TRex Bio filed to raise up to $100M in a Nasdaq IPO](https://kfgo.com/2026/09/18/retension-pharmaceuticals-trex-bio-file-for-us-ipo-as-biotech-momentum-continues/)** to fund Phase 1 autoimmune and inflammatory-disease programs. The filing is another test of whether the biotech IPO window can reopen for early clinical platforms.
- **[Researchers extended room-temperature quantum entanglement lifetime by up to 240×](https://phys.org/news/2026-09-nuclear-swap-room-temperature-entanglement.html)** by swapping electron-spin entanglement into a silicon-29 nucleus in silicon carbide. Longer-lived ambient-condition quantum memory could reduce cooling overhead and improve practical network-node designs.

## Watch List
- **Neura Robotics financing:** The company reportedly intends to seek up to €1B for humanoid production; track whether a round closes and at what valuation.
- **20–30 MW AI data centers:** Anthropic and OpenAI are reportedly pursuing smaller sites to bring capacity online faster; signed deals would broaden demand beyond gigawatt campuses.
- **Saudi oil redundancy:** Tests to restart the damaged East–West pipeline will show how quickly non-Hormuz export capacity can recover after attacks.
