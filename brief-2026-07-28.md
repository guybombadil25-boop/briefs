# Investment Brief — 2026-07-28

## AI Infrastructure
- **[Etched raised $300M at a $10.3B valuation](https://www.msn.com/en-us/money/companies/ai-chip-startup-etched-raises-300-million-at-103-billion-valuation/ar-AA28xklH) before shipping its transformer-optimized Sohu chip at scale.** The round validates demand for inference-specific silicon but also embeds substantial execution risk at a pre-volume stage.
- **[AMD launched its MI400 GPUs, Helios rack-scale system, sixth-generation EPYC CPUs and expanded software stack](https://markets.businessinsider.com/news/stocks/aai-2026-amd-delivers-full-stack-compute-for-the-agentic-ai-era-1036359541).** A credible rack-level alternative to Nvidia would shift competition from individual accelerators toward integrated compute, networking and software economics.
- **[Nvidia is reportedly behind a $50B lease for Hut 8’s Texas data center](https://www.ft.com/content/685014e7-47dd-471b-a585-1b9b73ce5d6f).** Chip vendors financing capacity and taking lease risk would mark a major vertical-integration shift—and raise balance-sheet exposure if AI demand undershoots.

## Robotics
- **[Enigma emerged from stealth with a $70M seed round](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/27/enigma-raises-70m-to-make-controlling-a-robot-as-easy-as-adjusting-the-volume/) and more than 100 remotely controllable robots for gathering human-interaction data.** Its crowdsourced control layer targets a key physical-AI bottleneck: diverse, real-world training data rather than another humanoid hardware platform.
- **[Foundation Robotics partnered with AMD to power its Phantom humanoids](https://www.msn.com/en-us/money/companies/eric-trump-backed-foundation-partners-with-amd-to-develop-humanoid-robots/ar-AA28y1dx).** The deal broadens the robotics compute stack beyond Nvidia and gives AMD a reference deployment in industrial and defense-oriented humanoids.

## Energy
- **[Antares raised a $470M Series C](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/27/antares-raises-470m-to-build-nuclear-reactors-for-the-u-s-military/) to deploy microreactors for U.S. military and space missions.** Defense-backed first customers could compress commercialization timelines for small reactors and de-risk later civilian deployments.
- **[Oklo received DOE startup authorization for its Groves isotope reactor](https://oklo.com/newsroom/news-details/2026/Oklo-Receives-U-S--Department-of-Energy-Startup-Authorization-for-Groves-Reactor-Clearing-Way-for-Fuel-Loading-and-First-Criticality/default.aspx), clearing fuel loading and first criticality.** Moving from paper approvals into operations is a meaningful proof point for faster advanced-nuclear licensing and execution.
- **[The EIB provided Dutch grid operator Enexis €500M in financing](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/dutch-grid-operator-enexis-gets-500-million-financing-eib-2026-07-28/) for distribution-grid expansion.** Large public financing is shifting toward the wires needed for electrification, reinforcing grid capex as the binding energy-transition bottleneck.

## Crypto
- **[BitMEX said it will shut down](https://www.reuters.com/business/cryptocurrency-exchange-bitmex-shut-down-2026-07-23/) as competition concentrates liquidity at larger exchanges.** The closure of a former derivatives leader signals that scale, regulatory access and product breadth are becoming harder infrastructure moats.
- **[Ripple launched Ripple Mint for institutional issuance and management of RLUSD](https://www.coindesk.com/business/2026/07/24/ripple-s-rlusd-gets-two-boosts-as-transfer-volume-drops-25).** Direct minting and treasury tooling move stablecoins deeper into enterprise workflows, though weak transfer-volume trends remain an adoption check.
- **[KB Kookmin Bank will launch 24/7 dollar trade payments across 10 countries on JPMorgan’s Kinexys network](https://www.crowdfundinsider.com/2026/07/293953-south-korean-kb-kookmin-bank-to-roll-out-cross-border-payments-via-jpmorgans-kinexys-platform/).** A top Korean bank moving corporate payments onchain is a stronger institutional-infrastructure signal than another crypto-native pilot.

## Emerging Tech
- **[Rocket Lab won a record $266M U.S. Space Force contract](https://www.space.com/space-exploration/launches-spacecraft/rocket-lab-to-start-launching-missile-defense-missions-from-alaska-under-usd266-million-space-force-deal) for up to 18 suborbital missions.** The award expands Rocket Lab’s national-security backlog and validates HASTE as infrastructure for high-cadence hypersonic testing.
- **[The FDA approved Outlook Therapeutics’ LYTENAVA for wet AMD](https://www.reuters.com/legal/litigation/us-fda-approves-outlook-therapeutics-eye-disease-drug-2026-07-24/), the first approved ophthalmic formulation of bevacizumab.** The decision opens a large market historically served by off-label compounded drugs and gives the company a differentiated regulatory position.
- **[The FDA approved GSK’s Jideytro ahead of schedule](https://www.msn.com/en-us/health/other/gsk-enters-lung-cancer-drug-market-with-early-fda-nod-for-jideytro/ar-AA28tkyz), its first lung-cancer medicine.** The accelerated approval quickly validates GSK’s recent $10.6B Nuvalent acquisition and strengthens the case for targeted oncology M&A.

## Watch List
- **The Exploration Company is reportedly seeking $300M** to accelerate spacecraft development; confirmation would be another large European space-infrastructure round.
- **The SEC’s promised crypto “innovation exemption” has not yet surfaced.** Scope and timing could materially alter U.S. fundraising and DeFi compliance economics.
- **Nvidia’s reported $50B Texas commitment:** Track final tenant, sublease and financing terms to determine whether chip suppliers are absorbing demand risk previously borne by hyperscalers.
