# Investment Brief — 2026-05-31

## AI Infrastructure
- **IREN signed a $1.6B Dell deal for Nvidia Blackwell systems** at its Texas data-center campus. This is another proof point that GPU-cloud demand is translating into hardware commitments, not just leasing headlines.
- **Nvidia used Computex 2026 to showcase Vera Rubin NVL72 and Taiwan AI-infrastructure partnerships.** The roadmap keeps pressure on the rest of the accelerator stack and reinforces Taiwan as the critical node for next-gen AI compute.
- **Cognition raised $1B at a $25B pre-money valuation.** Even though it is an AI coding company rather than hard infrastructure, the round is a strong signal that agentic software remains a major demand driver for inference and developer compute.

## Robotics
- **SoftBank hired banks for U.S. IPOs of SB Energy and autonomous-robotics spinout Roze**, with Roze focused on robotics for AI-infrastructure construction. If executed, it would create a public-market pure play at the intersection of robotics, data centers, and energy buildout.
- **Pudu Robotics disclosed 130,000 robots operating across 80+ countries and regions** while pitching a multi-embodiment robotics platform at BEYOND Expo. The deployment base matters because robotics winners are increasingly being valued on installed fleet data, not demos.

## Energy
- **Energy Vault closed its acquisition of an 850 MW battery-storage portfolio in Japan from BayWa r.e.** The deal gives it an immediate grid-scale operating platform in a market where renewables growth is constrained by storage and interconnection.
- **The U.S. DOE awarded more than $94M to eight companies to accelerate small modular reactors.** The dollars are modest, but the signal is regulatory and supply-chain de-risking for nuclear power tied to AI-load growth.
- **India’s 2026 energy investment is expected to hit $170B, led by solar, grid, and refining spend, according to IEA-linked reporting.** The scale keeps India near the center of grid-equipment, storage, and renewables supply-chain demand.

## Crypto
- **U.S. crypto groups mobilized for a Senate vote on the CLARITY/payment-stablecoin framework after a 15-9 bipartisan committee advance.** Floor progress would materially improve the regulatory backdrop for U.S. stablecoin issuance and market-structure infrastructure.
- **Base launched an AI-agent crypto tool as part of its layer-2 product expansion.** The move is a notable infrastructure signal: wallets and L2s are shifting toward agent execution layers rather than only consumer payments or trading.

## Emerging Tech
- **The U.S. government’s roughly $2B quantum-computing investment push continued to ripple through the sector**, including reported equity-style stakes across quantum firms and related foundry capacity. Direct government ownership/funding could compress timelines for domestic quantum hardware.
- **OpenAI-backed Chai raised $70M for AI-driven drug discovery.** The round supports the thesis that antibody and protein-design platforms are becoming investable AI-native biotech infrastructure, not single-asset biotech bets.
- **Blue Origin faces a months-long setback after a rocket explosion damaged its launch pad, according to Reuters-linked reporting.** That could tighten launch capacity for Amazon satellite schedules and strengthen SpaceX’s near-term pricing power.

## Watch List
- **Groq reportedly raising $650M for an AI inference-cloud pivot**: high-signal if confirmed by primary reporting; watch valuation, Nvidia relationship, and cloud capacity commitments.
- **SoftBank’s Roze IPO timeline**: a filing would reveal whether robotics-for-AI-buildout has real revenue or is still strategic narrative.
- **CLARITY Act floor vote**: passage or delay will set the next regulatory catalyst for U.S. stablecoin and market-structure plays.
