# Investment Brief — 2026-06-25

## AI Infrastructure
- **OpenAI and Broadcom unveiled “Jalapeño,” OpenAI’s first custom inference chip, with TSMC manufacturing and OpenAI targeting multi-gigawatt deployments.** This confirms frontier labs are moving from GPU rental economics toward vertically optimized silicon for cost-per-token and supply control.
- **SpaceX reportedly signed another AI-compute customer, Reflection AI, after recent GPU-capacity deals with Google/Anthropic.** Space-based or SpaceX-controlled compute is shifting from novelty to strategic capacity arbitrage as terrestrial data-center permitting and power constraints tighten.

## Robotics
- **Agility Robotics agreed to go public via a Churchill Capital SPAC at a $2.5B pre-money valuation, targeting roughly $620M of proceeds for Digit humanoid production.** This is the first major U.S. humanoid public-market test and will expose real backlog, unit economics, and warehouse-deployment cadence.
- **Faraday Future expanded its “EAI Robot World” lineup at Automate Chicago with the Faber mobile manipulator.** The signal is less FF-specific and more market-wide: auto/EV platforms are repackaging sensing, autonomy, and manufacturing assets into industrial robotics products.

## Energy
- **Canada released a national Nuclear Energy Strategy and advanced Darlington as the West’s first grid-scale SMR project.** Policy plus construction milestones compress the SMR debate from “will it be licensed?” to “can it be built on cost and schedule?”
- **Seven First Nations took a reported $715M equity stake in Canada’s first SMR project.** Indigenous-backed project finance could become a repeatable template for nuclear permitting and social-license risk reduction.
- **DOE highlighted $350M for coal plant modernization/restarts and continued nuclear-supply-chain acceleration.** The U.S. grid reliability posture is moving toward firm capacity, not just renewables-plus-storage, which matters for AI load growth.

## Crypto
- **Congress scheduled a July 17 New York hearing on the CLARITY Act as the market-structure bill faces a narrowing Senate path.** The hearing is a near-term catalyst for whether U.S. crypto infrastructure gets a CFTC/SEC boundary or remains litigation-led.
- **Law-enforcement groups pushed back on CLARITY Act safe-harbor language for non-custodial developers after the Roman Storm/Tornado Cash fallout.** Developer protections are becoming the key fault line for DeFi infrastructure investability.
- **Stablecoin reserve management is becoming a Wall Street product category under the GENIUS Act framework.** Fidelity/State Street-style reserve funds point to stablecoins becoming regulated cash-management infrastructure rather than purely crypto-native balance sheets.

## Emerging Tech
- **Insilico Medicine and SK Biopharmaceuticals signed an AI drug-discovery collaboration worth up to $2.5B for neuroimmune therapies.** Big, backloaded milestones validate pharma willingness to pay for AI-native discovery pipelines, while keeping proof risk tied to clinical conversion.
- **BenchSci raised about $50M to expand its AI-powered biomedical R&D platform.** The round reinforces that “AI for lab productivity” is attracting growth capital beyond molecule generation, especially in preclinical workflow compression.
- **SpaceX’s compute deal with Reflection AI blurs space infrastructure and AI infrastructure.** If Starlink/SpaceX can monetize orbital or network-adjacent compute, space assets become part of the AI supply chain rather than a standalone connectivity thesis.

## Watch List
- **Neura Robotics is reportedly seeking up to €1B/$1.2B after its recent mega-round.** Track whether this closes; it would reset humanoid valuation comps in Europe.
- **CLARITY Act July hearing and Senate calendar.** Failure to advance before recess would push crypto market-structure certainty into a later window.
- **Quantum funding follow-through after Atom Computing’s reported $100M Series C.** Watch for government-backed fault-tolerance milestones translating into customer deployments, not just lab roadmaps.
