# Investment Brief — 2026-07-07

## AI Infrastructure
- **TeraWulf signed a 20-year AI data-campus lease with Anthropic for ~401 MW of critical IT load.** Hyperscale AI demand is increasingly being secured through long-duration power/data-center contracts, making energy access a core AI-infrastructure bottleneck.
- **Switch is reportedly seeking ~$2B at a nearly $50B valuation ahead of a potential IPO.** If priced, it would reinforce that institutional capital still wants scarce, powered data-center capacity despite concerns about AI capex digestion.
- **Blackstone-owned QTS withdrew its final appeal for a planned 22M-square-foot Virginia data-center campus.** The collapse of a would-be world-scale campus shows permitting, grid, and local-resistance risk can materially cap AI buildout supply.
- **Wisconsin utilities now forecast energy demand rising ~40% by 2032, largely driven by three hyperscale data centers.** Regional grid planning is becoming a leading indicator for where AI infrastructure can actually scale.

## Robotics
- **Unitree Robotics won Chinese approval for a ~$618M Shanghai STAR Market IPO.** A public listing would create a major valuation benchmark for profitable embodied-AI/humanoid companies and deepen China’s robotics capital-market lead.
- **Apptronik opened its Austin “Robot Park” training hub and unveiled Apollo 2.** The move shifts humanoid competition from demos toward real-world data collection, fleet training, and deployable task performance.
- **Agility Robotics’ SPAC path continued to draw scrutiny as the first likely U.S.-listed pure-play humanoid equity.** Public-market access could fund scaling, but filings and CEO commentary will pressure the sector to show revenue, margins, and deployable use cases.
- **Tesla’s Optimus ramp expectations were reset lower, with Musk warning production will be “extremely slow” initially.** That tempers near-term humanoid hype and raises the bar for manufacturability proof from all players.

## Energy
- **Deployable Energy’s Unity demonstration reactor reportedly reached criticality at Idaho.** A private advanced-reactor criticality milestone supports the thesis that U.S. nuclear timelines are compressing under federal support.
- **The NRC proposed a major reactor-licensing overhaul in a 553-page rulemaking.** Streamlined licensing could be more important than single-project funding for nuclear commercialization if it reduces approval uncertainty.
- **WeLight announced a $650M solar mini-grid expansion for off-grid communities in Nigeria.** Large distributed-renewables capital is moving into emerging-market grid gaps, where demand growth and weak transmission make mini-grids investable infrastructure.
- **European battery-storage and grid investments continue to surge, including new Polish BESS portfolio activity.** Storage is increasingly the clearing mechanism for renewables penetration and data-center/grid reliability.

## Crypto
- **The CLARITY Act missed its July 4 target and stalled in the Senate over ethics, DeFi, and stablecoin-yield disputes.** U.S. market-structure clarity remains delayed, extending regulatory uncertainty for exchanges, token issuers, and DeFi infrastructure.
- **Jefferies and Galaxy flagged rising odds of crypto-market volatility as the CLARITY Act faces a compressed Senate calendar.** Passage could unlock institutional adoption; delay pushes the next decision point toward the August window.
- **FairShake and affiliated crypto PACs entered the 2026 midterm cycle with a reported $193M war chest led by Ripple, Coinbase, and a16z.** Crypto policy is becoming a durable, well-funded lobbying vertical, which may matter as much as litigation for regulatory outcomes.
- **Yield Guild Games shut down its crypto game-publishing arm and laid off 35 staff while pivoting toward AI.** Gaming/consumer crypto remains weak; infrastructure and policy exposure look higher signal than app-layer speculation.

## Emerging Tech
- **IQM Quantum Computers completed its merger with Real Asset Acquisition Corp. and began trading on Nasdaq as IQMX on July 2.** Europe now has a public quantum-computing pure play, adding liquid comps for quantum hardware valuations.
- **Alice & Bob is investing €50M in a Paris quantum lab and nanofabrication cleanroom.** Quantum hardware companies are moving capex into proprietary fabrication, a sign the sector is shifting from lab prototypes toward scaling infrastructure.
- **Inceptive reportedly raised $100M to apply AI to drug design.** AI-native biotech remains one of the few areas attracting large rounds despite a selective funding market, especially where platforms can compress discovery timelines.
- **Rocket Lab’s planned $8B Iridium acquisition continued to reshape public-space infrastructure positioning.** Vertical integration of launch, satellites, spectrum, and recurring connectivity revenue is becoming the strategic endgame for space platforms.

## Watch List
- **U.S. crypto August window:** Track whether CLARITY gets floor time after the July recess; a miss likely pushes market-structure clarity into 2027.
- **Data-center permitting backlash:** QTS/Virginia may not be isolated; watch local grid, water, and zoning pushback as a cap on AI compute supply.
- **Humanoid IPO disclosures:** Unitree and Agility filings should reveal the real spread between demo valuations and commercial robot economics.
