# Investment Brief — 2026-06-07

## AI Infrastructure
- **IREN closed a $3.65B investment-grade GPU financing** backed by its Microsoft AI cloud contract. This is a proof point that AI compute is becoming securitizable infrastructure, with contracted GPU capacity starting to look like project finance.
- **STMicroelectronics lifted its 2026 data-center revenue target to about $1B**, up from “nicely above $500M,” and guided toward nearly doubling again in 2027. AI infrastructure demand is broadening beyond GPUs into power, connectivity, and silicon-carbide/analog supply chains.
- **Google reportedly agreed to pay SpaceX roughly $920M per month for Nvidia GPU capacity at xAI data centers.** If confirmed, hyperscaler compute procurement is moving into mega-lease structures and validating nontraditional data-center owners.

## Robotics
- **Unitree cleared a key review for a Shanghai STAR Market IPO targeting roughly $618M.** A public humanoid listing would give investors real revenue/margin disclosure and reset comps for the physical-AI stack.
- **Pudu Robotics launched PuduFM 1.0 and PuduAgent**, a foundation-model and agent platform for embodied AI systems. The signal is that commercial robot vendors are shifting from hardware SKUs to software/control layers that could scale across fleets.
- **China’s humanoid commercialization cadence accelerated, with AGIBOT reportedly reaching 10,000 units.** Unit volumes matter more than demos now; supply-chain scale could pressure Western humanoid cost curves.

## Energy
- **Antares Nuclear’s Mark-0 microreactor reached zero-power criticality at Idaho National Laboratory under the DOE reactor pilot program.** This is a major timeline-compression signal for advanced nuclear and the first U.S. privately developed non-light-water reactor criticality milestone in decades.
- **NANO Nuclear’s University of Illinois KRONOS microreactor construction-permit application was accepted for NRC review.** University and industrial campuses are becoming the early deployment path for microreactors as AI loads strain grid interconnection timelines.
- **North Carolina’s House passed the Ratepayer Protection Act.** State-level rate design is becoming a bottleneck/opportunity for grid modernization, nuclear cost recovery, and utility willingness to serve AI data-center load.

## Crypto
- **The CLARITY Act was placed on the U.S. Senate legislative calendar after clearing Senate Banking 15–9.** Market-structure legislation moving to the floor is the clearest near-term catalyst for institutional crypto infrastructure, but timing remains hostage to the Senate calendar.
- **Big banks escalated opposition to crypto legislation, including stablecoin and market-structure provisions.** The fight is shifting from “whether crypto is legitimate” to who controls deposits, payments rails, and tokenized settlement.
- **Radiant Capital shut down after a $50M DeFi hack.** DeFi security remains a thesis limiter: protocols with weak risk controls can lose institutional trust even as regulation improves.

## Emerging Tech
- **NewLimit raised $435M for longevity/epigenetic reprogramming after reporting age-reversal signals in human liver cells.** The round moves aging biology from speculative science toward clinic-ready assets, with Founders Fund-scale capital behind it.
- **IMU Biosciences closed a $53M Series A to scale high-definition immune profiling.** Large immune datasets plus AI could create defensible diagnostic and drug-discovery infrastructure if the platform proves clinical utility.
- **Impulse Space raised $500M at a reported ~$4.3B valuation.** “Last-mile” orbital infrastructure is becoming a venture-scale market as maneuverable spacecraft become core to defense, comms, and in-space logistics.
- **Quantinuum listed on Nasdaq under QNT in a closely watched quantum-computing debut.** A new public-market benchmark should sharpen valuation discipline across quantum hardware, software, and error-correction plays.

## Watch List
- **Google–SpaceX/xAI GPU lease:** verify contract terms and counterparty exposure; if real, it may reprice AI data-center capacity globally.
- **CLARITY Act floor timing:** a failed June/July window could push comprehensive U.S. crypto market structure out years.
- **Antares follow-on milestones:** watch whether the DOE pilot converts zero-power criticality into repeatable licensing/deployment speed for commercial microreactors.
