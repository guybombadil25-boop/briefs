# Investment Brief — 2026-05-13

## AI Infrastructure
- **Hut 8 drew renewed attention for a 15-year, $9.8B AI data-center lease at its Beacon project.** The deal reinforces the thesis that power access and long-duration capacity contracts are becoming as investable as GPUs themselves.
- **Vistra is pursuing Cogentrix Energy’s 5.5 GW gas portfolio while expanding AI power relationships.** Gas-backed dispatchable generation is emerging as a near-term bridge for hyperscaler load growth before nuclear and grid upgrades arrive.
- **Vapi raised a $50M Series B for voice-AI infrastructure.** Voice agents are moving from application layer novelty toward core AI workflow infrastructure, with developer platforms capturing usage as inference costs fall.

## Robotics
- **Genesis AI debuted GENE-26.5 after its $105M seed round, showing a full-stack foundation-model-plus-robotic-hand demo.** The signal is that robotics foundation models are shifting from simulation claims to dexterity benchmarks that can attract generalist AI capital.
- **Omron Robotics and Comau signed a strategic industrial-automation partnership.** Cross-vendor automation alliances matter because factories increasingly want integrated cells rather than single-vendor robot deployments.
- **Mitsubishi Electric reportedly led a $140M round in Lumos Robotics.** Strategic capital from an automation incumbent suggests embodied AI is being pulled into manufacturing infrastructure, not just humanoid demos.

## Energy
- **Duke Energy highlighted a $103B capital plan.** Utility capex is increasingly an AI-and-electrification bottleneck thesis: winners may be regulated utilities and grid equipment suppliers that can earn on massive rate-base expansion.
- **Germany advanced a hydrogen transport mandate that Provaris flagged as a catalyst.** Dedicated hydrogen logistics policy could accelerate investable midstream infrastructure if offtake and shipping standards converge.
- **Frontier Power USA launched as a long-duration storage development and investment platform.** New storage platforms are forming around the gap between renewable penetration and AI/data-center demand for firm power.

## Crypto
- **The U.S. Senate Banking Committee set a May 14 markup for the CLARITY Act after a stablecoin-yield compromise.** A real market-structure bill would be thesis-changing for exchanges, custodians, and token issuers by clarifying SEC/CFTC boundaries.
- **Aptos Foundation and Aptos Labs committed over $50M to on-chain trading, AI agents, and infrastructure.** L1 ecosystems are increasingly funding full-stack institutional tooling rather than relying solely on generic DeFi incentives.
- **SUI Group is pulling back from DeFi exposure after hacks while targeting 3–4% yield by year-end.** Treasury and risk-management discipline is becoming a differentiator as institutions evaluate DeFi infrastructure.

## Emerging Tech
- **Quantum Motion raised $160M Series C to commercialize silicon-based quantum computing.** The round supports the thesis that manufacturable silicon approaches may attract late-stage capital before fault-tolerant systems are fully proven.
- **Skyroot reportedly became India’s first spacetech unicorn as it prepares Vikram-1.** India’s private launch market is moving from policy promise to venture-scale companies with orbital-launch milestones.
- **Banyan BioInnovations launched with $100M and an ICON collaboration.** Biotech company formation is leaning into platform-plus-services models that can compress development cycles and share infrastructure risk.

## Watch List
- **Agnikul Cosmos funding talks:** reports indicate a possible $50–75M round at a roughly $500M valuation; track whether it closes and how quickly capital converts into launch cadence.
- **CLARITY Act markup outcome:** watch amendments on stablecoin yield, custody, and SEC/CFTC jurisdiction; small wording changes could move the investability of U.S. crypto infrastructure.
- **Humanoid deployment claims:** monitor whether BMW/Figure, 1X, and Chinese suppliers publish paid-deployment economics rather than unit-capacity targets.
