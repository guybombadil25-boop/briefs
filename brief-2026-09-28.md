# Investment Brief — 2026-09-28

## AI Infrastructure
- **[Nscale raised $3.36B in pre-IPO convertible financing](https://www.nscale.com/press-releases/pre-ipo-convertible-financing),** led by Third Point with a $1B follow-on commitment from Nvidia. The unusually large bridge gives the neocloud capital to accelerate global data-center construction while testing public-market appetite for GPU infrastructure.
- **[CleanSpark closed $2.276B of senior secured notes](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/cleanspark-inc-announces-closing-of-2-276-billion-of-senior-secured-notes-302890605.html)** for its Georgia data-center subsidiary. Asset-backed debt at this scale shows AI campuses are developing a project-finance market distinct from speculative corporate funding.

## Robotics
- **[Amazon committed more than $100M to a new Indiana robotics-manufacturing plant](https://press.aboutamazon.com/job-creation-and-investment/2026/9/amazon-to-create-300-high-paying-jobs-at-new-advanced-manufacturing-facility-in-greenwood-indiana),** scheduled to begin operations in 2028 and double its U.S. robotics-production capacity. Verticalizing hardware supply after deploying over one million robots signals sustained internal automation demand.
- **[Intrinsic open-sourced its core industrial-robotics stack](https://roboticsandautomationnews.com/2026/09/23/intrinsic-open-sources-core-industrial-robotics-technology/105036/)** as ROS-compatible building blocks for motion, perception, and orchestration. Alphabet’s move could lower integration costs and establish a common software layer for physical-AI developers.

## Energy
- **[The U.S. announced a multinational civil-nuclear consortium targeting more than $150B of European reactor investment](https://www.state.gov/releases/office-of-the-spokesman/2026/09/the-united-states-announces-civil-nuclear-initiatives-on-the-margins-of-the-united-nations-general-assembly/).** Coordinating U.S., Japanese, Korean, and Polish suppliers could turn geopolitical support into a bankable deployment pipeline for Western nuclear vendors.
- **[Crusoe ended a planned $1.25B purchase of Boom Supersonic gas turbines](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/25/crusoe-abandons-1-25b-plan-to-use-boom-turbines-at-ai-data-centers/)** for AI data centers. The cancellation is an execution warning for unproven behind-the-meter generation and leaves a potentially large power gap for Crusoe’s buildout.

## Crypto
- **[The Federal Reserve proposed GENIUS Act rules requiring 1:1 reserves and standardized capital for supervised stablecoin issuers](https://www.theblock.co/news/regulation/2026-09-24-fed-proposes-reserve-limits-capital-standards-stablecoin-issuers-genius-act-416336).** Moving from statute to implementable prudential rules gives banks clearer economics while raising the compliance barrier for smaller issuers.
- **[Bitget said North Korea was likely behind its roughly $352M breach](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/25/crypto-platform-bitget-suspects-north-korea-in-352-million-hack.html).** Confirmation near the high end of early loss estimates makes exchange key management and reserve transparency a renewed infrastructure risk, not merely an isolated exploit.

## Emerging Tech
- **[Hubble Network raised a $200M Series C at a $1.6B valuation and opened satellite coverage to ordinary Bluetooth LE devices](https://hubble.com/news/hubble-network-opens-satellite-coverage-to-all-bluetooth-devices-raises-200-million-series-c-at-1.6-billion-valuation).** Firmware-only connectivity could dramatically reduce satellite-IoT hardware costs; proceeds support expansion from six satellites toward a 60-spacecraft constellation.
- **[Redwire was selected for the Space Force’s $980M NITE-STAR contract vehicle](https://rdw.com/newsroom/redwire-selected-for-space-systems-commands-980-million-nite-star-idiq-contract/).** The multi-vendor award carries no guaranteed revenue, but positions Redwire to compete for national-security space-test infrastructure and sustainment work.

## Watch List
- **Nscale IPO:** Track pricing, conversion terms, and whether public investors validate the financing’s implied economics.
- **Crusoe power replacement:** Watch which technology replaces the canceled Boom turbines and whether data-center energization slips.
- **Bitget recovery:** Monitor independently verified losses, reserve coverage, asset recovery, and formal attribution to North Korea.
