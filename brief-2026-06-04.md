# Investment Brief — 2026-06-04

## AI Infrastructure
- **ZutaCore raised $100M Series C** to scale waterless, direct-to-chip liquid cooling for AI data centers. Cooling is becoming a gating bottleneck for dense GPU clusters, and retrofit-friendly thermal tech can capture spend before new power capacity arrives.
- **NVIDIA said Vera Rubin infrastructure is now in production at Computex 2026** and is pushing DGX Station into Windows workflows. This tightens the accelerator cadence and signals NVIDIA wants more of the full-stack enterprise AI workstation/data-center bridge.
- **Perplexity unveiled a hybrid local-cloud inference orchestrator at Computex.** Dynamic routing between edge and cloud inference is an early signal that cost, latency, and data-sovereignty constraints are becoming product architecture—not just infra procurement—decisions.

## Robotics
- **Apptronik reportedly raised $520M to scale Apollo humanoid robots.** If confirmed at that size, it would mark one of the largest private bets on near-term humanoid manufacturing capacity and enterprise deployment.
- **Mecka AI secured $60M for robotics training data, per startup funding roundups.** The pick-and-shovel layer for physical AI—teleoperation data, simulation, and evaluation—may compound faster than any single robot OEM.
- **YY Group launched a Singapore humanoid robot training lab powered by NVIDIA.** The move is more infrastructure than product revenue today, but it reinforces Asia’s push to build standardized training pipelines for service and facility-management robots.

## Energy
- **Maxwell Power landed a $750M capital commitment for solar and battery storage financing.** The deal shows capital is still flowing to grid-scale storage despite policy noise, with financing platforms becoming a key bottleneck unlock for distributed energy buildout.
- **German BESS operator Terralayr raised €192M to expand its flexibility platform.** Large-scale battery optimization is moving from project-by-project development into infrastructure software plus asset aggregation.
- **Day & Zimmermann partnered with Deep Fission to build underground small modular reactors.** The announcement adds a major EPC/infrastructure partner to a novel nuclear deployment model, worth tracking as AI load growth keeps pulling nuclear timelines forward.

## Crypto
- **The CLARITY Act moved onto the U.S. Senate calendar after clearing committee.** A floor vote would be a thesis-changing regulatory step, splitting SEC/CFTC jurisdiction and potentially reopening U.S. institutional crypto infrastructure investment.
- **Securitize launched a tokenized private-credit fund on TRON with Hamilton Lane exposure.** Tokenized real-world assets keep moving from pilots toward distribution plumbing, with private credit remaining one of the clearest institutional use cases.
- **Radiant Capital is winding down after failing to recover from a roughly $50M hack.** The shutdown is a reminder that DeFi security and cross-chain risk remain existential, even for protocols that survive the initial exploit.
- **Onchain stock trading reportedly reached $5.5B in volume across 2.8M wallets.** The signal is still early and fragmented, but equity tokenization is gaining enough activity to matter for market-structure infrastructure.

## Emerging Tech
- **OQC raised £260M / roughly $350M Series C, billed as Europe’s largest private quantum round.** Quantum is shifting from research optionality to sovereign and enterprise infrastructure competition.
- **IBM committed more than $10B over five years to quantum computing.** The spend covers R&D, manufacturing, M&A, and ecosystem expansion, raising the bar for fault-tolerant quantum roadmaps and making smaller pure plays more acquisition-relevant.
- **Microsoft upgraded its topological quantum chip, while Nature noted researchers remain skeptical.** The upside is a faster scaling path if validated; the risk is that headline quantum roadmaps still need independent reproducibility before underwriting.
- **F2G raised $100M to refile its antifungal candidate olorofim.** Late-stage infectious-disease biotech remains capital-intensive but can re-rate quickly when regulatory setbacks turn into approvable paths.

## Watch List
- **CLARITY Act floor timing:** Watch whether Senate leadership schedules a vote or the ethics/stablecoin fight delays market-structure legislation again.
- **Apptronik round confirmation and customer disclosures:** The $520M humanoid financing signal needs primary-source confirmation and deployment economics.
- **Fusion/nuclear rulemaking:** NRC fusion-framework activity and novel SMR partnerships could compress timelines if regulators create repeatable licensing pathways.
