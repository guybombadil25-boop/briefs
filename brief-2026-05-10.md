# Investment Brief — 2026-05-10

## AI Infrastructure
- **Panthalassa raised a $140M Series B led by Peter Thiel** to build wave-powered offshore AI data centers, with reports putting valuation near $1B. If viable, it attacks the two hardest AI-infra bottlenecks at once: power availability and data-center siting.
- **Major infrastructure investors are circling a reported ~$900B data-center capex opportunity**, with Blackstone, Blue Owl and peers highlighted as active capital providers. The signal: AI infra is shifting from hyperscaler balance sheets into dedicated real-asset financing markets.
- **MaxLinear launched Panther V at Dell Tech World ’26** for AI-inference data movement and latency reduction inside data centers. Interconnect and memory-movement efficiency remain under-owned leverage points as inference workloads scale.
- **TSMC reported strong April sales tied to AI semiconductor demand**, reinforcing that HBM, advanced packaging and leading-edge capacity remain the near-term choke points. Watch whether demand breadth extends beyond Nvidia-linked supply chains.

## Robotics
- **Linkerbot is targeting a $6B valuation in its next round**, with Reuters reporting the Chinese robotic-hand maker controls >80% of the high-dexterity robotic-hand market. Dexterous manipulation is becoming a scarce-enabling layer for humanoids, not just an end-effector component.
- **Morgan Stanley flagged humanoids as a driver of China’s next export wave**, citing China’s early lead in the category. The thesis implication is geopolitical: humanoid supply chains may rhyme with EVs/solar, where Chinese manufacturing scale compressed global costs quickly.

## Energy
- **ADB announced a $70B Asia-Pacific energy and digital infrastructure plan.** This is large enough to move grid, power and data-center enabling assets across emerging Asia, where AI and electrification demand are colliding.
- **The EU opened a €704M funding call for cross-border energy projects.** Transmission and interconnection remain the highest-leverage bottlenecks for renewable penetration and grid resilience.
- **Trench Group won a role in a U.S. 765 kV grid buildout tied to 4,000+ miles of new lines and 50–70 substations.** Ultra-high-voltage equipment is moving from niche utility capex into a strategic AI/electrification supply-chain constraint.

## Crypto
- **A stablecoin-legislation compromise reportedly lifted Circle shares 18%.** Market reaction suggests investors see U.S. stablecoin clarity as a direct unlock for public-market payment-infrastructure valuations.
- **Stablecoin transfers reportedly hit $4.5T in Q1 2026**, with payment use cases growing beyond trading flows. This supports the thesis that stablecoins are becoming settlement infrastructure, not just crypto liquidity plumbing.
- **CoinDesk reported executives see AI agents and large corporates as the next stablecoin demand drivers.** If autonomous software needs programmable payments, stablecoins could become default machine-to-machine transaction rails.
- **Spanish banks are joining a euro-stablecoin consortium push.** Europe is responding to dollar-stablecoin dominance with bank-led infrastructure, which could create regional winners under clearer MiCA rails.

## Emerging Tech
- **Blackstone Life Sciences invested $250M in Anagram Therapeutics** to advance an oral enzyme-replacement therapy for pancreatic insufficiency. The round shows large alternative-asset managers are still underwriting platform-like biotech risk when patient burden and trial path is clear.
- **Astranis raised a reported $200M Series D for its Omega satellite program.** Dedicated small GEO satellites remain a capital-intensive but strategically important alternative to broadband mega-constellations.
- **Crunchbase’s weekly funding screen showed enterprise AI, space tech and biotech leading the largest rounds.** The cluster matters because late-stage private capital is still concentrating in hard-tech/regulated categories despite broader venture selectivity.

## Watch List
- **Panthalassa pilot execution:** Track whether wave-powered compute nodes can hit uptime, maintenance and bandwidth targets offshore; the financing is real, but the operating model is unproven.
- **U.S. stablecoin bill timing:** Watch whether compromise language survives into final legislation and how yield restrictions affect Circle, banks and fintech issuers.
- **Humanoid dexterity supply chain:** Linkerbot’s next financing terms and customer concentration will indicate whether robotic hands become a bottleneck category comparable to batteries or lidar in prior hardware waves.
