# Investment Brief — 2026-10-13

## AI Infrastructure
- **Microsoft unveiled Maia 300 and plans [multi-gigawatt deployment](https://www.msn.com/en-ca/technology/artificial-intelligence/microsoft-plans-huge-deployment-of-its-own-ai-chips-with-an-eye-on-saving-costs/ar-AA2e4mAd?ocid=BingNewsVerp) from 2027.** Claimed 40% better tokens per megawatt makes custom inference silicon a direct lever on Nvidia dependence and power-constrained AI economics.

- **AMD launched [Helion](https://www.amd.com/en.html), an open rack-scale blueprint supporting up to 32,768 CPU cores for agentic-AI workloads.** Backing from eight systems vendors pushes AMD beyond chips into an interoperable infrastructure standard for high-volume inference.

- **Scaleway announced Europe’s first commercial [AMD MI355X AI supercomputer](https://www.reuters.com/business/media-telecom/scaleway-build-amd-powered-ai-supercomputer-sweden-2026-10-13/) in Sweden and a separate Nvidia site in Copenhagen.** The expansion adds sovereign European compute capacity while validating multi-vendor demand outside hyperscalers.

## Robotics
- **The U.S. Coast Guard placed a [$247.8 million order](https://breakingdefense.com/2026/10/coast-guard-places-248-million-order-for-first-mq-9b-drones/) for its first four MQ-9B long-range unmanned aircraft.** A major civil-service deployment broadens autonomous systems demand beyond defense and into persistent maritime surveillance and rescue.

- **South Korea cleared [20 humanoid robots for factory trials](https://en.sedaily.com/finance/2026/10/13/korea-clears-first-factory-test-of-20-humanoid-robots) after easing safety rules for stationary robots.** Trials across semiconductor, auto, shipbuilding and logistics sites create a regulated pathway from demos to production-floor validation.

## Energy
- **FERC approved PJM’s [50-MW-plus data-center registry](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/us-panel-approves-pjms-registry-data-centers-rejects-plan-curtail-power-them-2026-10-12/) but rejected preferential curtailment of new large loads.** Better demand visibility helps planning, while the ruling leaves unresolved who funds generation for the largest U.S. grid’s AI load growth.

- **Asian governments are accelerating oil stockpiles, a regional power grid and [renewables investment](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/asia-races-stockpile-oil-speed-up-renewables-response-iran-war-2026-10-08/) after Iran-war supply disruptions.** Energy security is becoming an additional policy driver for transmission and clean-generation capex, not merely decarbonization.

## Crypto
- **Samsung will add native USDC access to Wallet for [82 million eligible U.S. Galaxy devices](https://www.samsungmobilepress.com/articles/samsung-wallet-stablecoin-galaxy-us), with Coinbase and Bastion supplying custody infrastructure.** The late-October rollout embeds stablecoin transfers into mainstream hardware distribution rather than requiring a crypto-native app.

- **ClearToken and Consensys partnered on [24/7 wholesale settlement](https://www.marketsmedia.com/cleartoken-consensys-bring-24-7-wholesale-settlement-infrastructure/) inside the Bank of England’s Digital Securities Sandbox.** Combining regulated market infrastructure with public blockchain rails could shorten settlement cycles for tokenized and traditional assets.

## Emerging Tech
- **Aspen Neuroscience filed to raise up to [$100 million in a Nasdaq IPO](https://www.renaissancecapital.com/IPO-Center/News/122161/parkinson%E2%80%99s-disease-biotech-aspen-neuroscience-files-for-a-100-million-ipo).** The filing gives public investors a rare pure-play route into patient-specific stem-cell therapy for Parkinson’s, while testing risk appetite for early clinical biotech.

- **IonQ demonstrated a [1-kHz photonic interconnect](https://www.ionq.com/news/ionq-demonstrates-world-first-quantum-memory-enhanced-interconnect-for-distributed-quantum-applications) between a trapped-ion qubit and solid-state quantum memory.** The reported 1,000-plus entanglements per second targets a core bottleneck in networking modular quantum processors, though independent validation remains important.

- **NASA issued a final [request for proposals for commercial space stations](https://www.nasa.gov/news-release/nasa-seeks-us-industry-plans-for-commercial-space-stations/).** Moving procurement toward privately operated successors to the ISS creates a concrete demand signal for orbital infrastructure providers.

## Watch List
- **Faraday Future scheduled a joint robotics [product-launch event for October 14](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/ff-eai-robotics-ecosystem-inc-nasdaq-ffr-highlights-faraday-future-announcement-this-weeks-investor-weekly-report-from-yt-jia-rescheduled-to-october-14-ffr-and-ffai-to-host-joint-new-product-launch-event-on-october-14-302905227.html).** Track whether it reveals production-ready hardware, customers and manufacturing economics rather than another roadmap.

- **PJM must return with a lawful large-load framework after FERC rejected its curtailment plan.** The replacement will determine whether data centers bring generation, accept reliability limits or shift grid-upgrade costs to ratepayers.
