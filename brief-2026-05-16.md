# Investment Brief — 2026-05-16

## AI Infrastructure
- **Cerebras priced a $5.5B IPO and reportedly doubled on its Nasdaq debut.** This gives public-market validation to a pure-play AI silicon alternative to Nvidia and may reopen the exit window for capital-intensive chip startups.
- **Cowboy Space raised a $275M Series B led by Index Ventures to build orbital AI data centers.** The round pushes the AI-infrastructure bottleneck into power, cooling, and launch economics, with space-based solar compute becoming a venture-scale experiment.
- **Firmus Technologies raised A$500M (~US$325M) for green AI infrastructure in Australia.** Sovereign, power-constrained compute remains fundable when paired with renewable energy and data-center localization.
- **Nvidia reportedly committed more than $40B to AI equity deals in early 2026, including a large OpenAI stake.** The chip vendor is increasingly financing demand and supply-chain lock-in, which could reshape margins and customer dependence across the AI stack.

## Robotics
- **Humanoid signed a binding phased supply agreement with Schaeffler to deploy thousands of humanoid robots in German factories.** The first phase begins in late 2026/early 2027, moving humanoids from demos toward scaled industrial operations.
- **Richtech Robotics and SoundHound AI announced a live voice-enabled service-robot showcase for the National Restaurant Association Show.** If voice-to-robot ordering works reliably in hospitality, it tightens the loop between conversational AI and labor-replacement robotics.
- **Mind Robotics’ late-April $400M round at a reported $3.4B valuation continued to anchor the industrial-robot funding cycle.** The signal to watch is whether large factory-focused rounds convert into paid deployments rather than more teleoperated pilots.

## Energy
- **Hydrostor launched the first 4 GWh phase of its Quinte advanced compressed-air storage project in Ontario.** Long-duration storage moving from pilots to multi-GWh projects is critical for renewables-heavy grids and AI-load balancing.
- **Japan awarded 1.25 GW of battery storage in its latest long-term decarbonization capacity auction.** Capacity-market support is becoming a key revenue backstop for grid storage developers, even as auction rules pressure project economics.
- **National Grid outlined a five-year investment plan centered on grid expansion.** Regulated transmission and distribution capex remains one of the clearest ways to underwrite electrification, data-center growth, and renewables interconnection.
- **Nano Nuclear highlighted progress toward a construction permit for its Kronos micro modular reactor.** Microreactor milestones matter because AI/data-center demand is pulling nuclear from policy theme into customer-driven power procurement.

## Crypto
- **The CLARITY Act cleared the Senate Banking Committee in a 15–9 bipartisan vote.** A credible market-structure path splitting SEC/CFTC oversight would reduce U.S. regulatory overhang for exchanges, token issuers, and institutional infrastructure.
- **J.P. Morgan Asset Management launched its second tokenized money-market fund on Ethereum.** Large-bank RWA products are shifting tokenization from proofs of concept into yield-bearing institutional rails.
- **Ethereum Foundation unstaked roughly $49.6M of ETH for treasury rebalancing.** Foundation liquidity management is not thesis-changing alone, but it is a useful signal for ecosystem funding discipline and market-supply sensitivity.

## Emerging Tech
- **Oruka Therapeutics completed an upsized ~$700M follow-on offering after a sharp stock rally.** Public biotech capital is available for differentiated immunology assets, reinforcing the barbell of large late-stage raises and weak small-cap breadth.
- **D-Wave reported Q1 bookings of $33.4M, up from $1.6M a year earlier, including a $20M university-system sale.** Quantum remains speculative, but bookings are a better commercialization signal than headline qubit counts.
- **BlackSky said it secured more than two dozen Gen-3 on-demand customers in Q1 and raised its 2026 revenue outlook.** Real-time satellite intelligence demand is broadening beyond defense headlines into recurring geospatial subscriptions.
- **Havoc raised a $100M Series A for autonomous systems.** Defense-adjacent autonomy continues to attract large early rounds as investors price in drone, maritime, and robotic systems demand.

## Watch List
- **Space-based AI compute:** Track Cowboy Space launch milestones and payload economics; failure would reset enthusiasm for orbital data centers.
- **U.S. crypto legislation floor vote:** CLARITY’s committee win matters only if Senate floor timing and final SEC/CFTC boundaries hold.
- **Humanoid deployment proof:** Schaeffler’s first live factory deployments should produce real utilization, safety, and cost-per-task data by 2027.
