# Investment Brief — 2026-05-25

## AI Infrastructure
- **Modal Labs raised $355M at a $4.65B valuation** as demand for serverless AI compute keeps accelerating. The round is a clean signal that investors are still underwriting GPU abstraction layers, not just physical data-center capacity.
- **Google and Blackstone formed a new AI data-center venture around TPU access**, with CRN/Yahoo reporting the platform on May 20. Hyperscaler silicon is becoming an investable infrastructure product rather than an internal-only advantage.
- **Anthropic reportedly locked in a $1.25B/month compute contract with xAI through 2029.** If confirmed, frontier-model economics are shifting from model differentiation toward long-duration compute reservation and power access.
- **Etched reportedly raised $120M to pursue Nvidia-challenger AI chips.** Specialized inference silicon remains fundable where the pitch is lower cost per token, but proof will come from production workloads rather than benchmark claims.

## Robotics
- **Doozy Robotics announced global expansion across the U.S., GCC, and Asia with seed backing and live deployments.** The signal is not round size; it is that humanoid vendors are moving toward RaaS-style industrial workforce contracts ahead of larger Series A financings.
- **Waymo suspended freeway rides and paused Atlanta operations after safety issues, according to NBC/LA Times reports.** Autonomy scaling remains constrained by safety/regulatory trust, a reminder that deployment density can reverse quickly after edge-case failures.
- **Hyundai’s robotics buildout remains a major capacity marker**, with reports citing a U.S. robotics facility targeting 30,000 humanoid units annually by 2028. Watch whether humanoid supply chains scale like automotive programs or stall at pilot-line complexity.

## Energy
- **Canada committed C$2.2B to renew Chalk River Laboratories**, the country’s national nuclear research site. Public nuclear infrastructure spend is becoming strategic industrial policy as AI load growth reframes baseload power.
- **NANO Nuclear’s KRONOS MMR construction-permit application was accepted for U.S. NRC review at the University of Illinois.** Early NRC movement on microreactors matters because licensing velocity is the gating variable for distributed nuclear.
- **Fluence raised $420M through a follow-on equity offering for grid storage expansion.** Capital markets are rewarding storage platforms positioned to absorb AI/data-center volatility and renewable intermittency.
- **Ohio lawmakers moved to redefine “clean energy” to include natural gas for data-center power demand.** The policy shift shows AI load growth is already bending clean-energy definitions toward reliability-first grid politics.

## Crypto
- **The CLARITY Act continues to drive global crypto-policy positioning after Senate Banking approval.** The core issue is whether the U.S. creates a durable SEC/CFTC split that lets tokenization, DeFi, and stablecoin infrastructure operate inside mainstream finance.
- **Trump Media pulled SEC applications for spot Bitcoin and Ethereum ETFs.** The takeaway is that crypto ETF access is broadening, but issuer quality, conflicts, and disclosure standards remain meaningful regulatory filters.
- **Stablecoin and crypto firms are exploring bank-charter paths as policy clarity improves.** If charters become viable, the investable stack shifts from offshore exchanges toward regulated issuance, custody, payments, and tokenized cash infrastructure.

## Emerging Tech
- **SpaceX’s IPO filing reportedly highlighted heavy AI and Starship investment, including $3B of 2025 Starship R&D and $930M in Q1 2026.** A public SpaceX would reset valuation comps for launch, satellite broadband, and defense-space infrastructure.
- **GlobalFoundries launched Quantum Technology Solutions to scale U.S. quantum hardware manufacturing.** Quantum is moving from lab prototypes toward foundry/process-integration bottlenecks, which could create picks-and-shovels exposure before fault-tolerant machines arrive.
- **Rigetti reportedly secured $100M in U.S. government quantum-computing funding.** Government-backed quantum infrastructure remains one of the few credible near-term demand sources while commercial workloads mature.

## Watch List
- **CLARITY Act amendments:** Track final DeFi, stablecoin-yield, and developer-protection language; small wording changes could materially alter U.S. crypto infrastructure formation.
- **Microreactor licensing velocity:** KRONOS/NRC milestones and HALEU supply agreements will show whether advanced nuclear can compress timelines enough for data-center buyers.
- **SpaceX IPO timing and disclosures:** Watch whether the filing becomes a real liquidity event or a controlled secondary-market signal; either path will affect late-stage space valuations.
