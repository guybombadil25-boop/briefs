# Investment Brief — 2026-09-13

## AI Infrastructure
- **[Nvidia and eight Australian partners targeted up to 2 GW of AI-factory capacity by 2027](https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/nvidia-teams-up-with-australian-partners-build-ai-factory-capacity-2026-09-10/)** around its new DSX full-stack platform. The buildout would more than double Australia’s current data-center load and packages land, power, facilities, and compute as one deployment model.
- **[India’s Yotta outlined more than $12B of Nvidia GPU investment](https://www.moneycontrol.com/news/business/information-technology/yotta-plans-40-000-vera-rubin-gpus-for-new-noida-data-centre-eyes-12-billion-investment-14026856.html)**, including 40,000 Vera Rubin GPUs at a new 120-MW Noida site and 40,000 GB300s in Mumbai. Execution would make Yotta Nvidia’s largest Indian customer and materially expand sovereign AI capacity.
- **[DISA opened bidding for up to $21.6B of successor cloud contracts](https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/disa-launches-tender-for-216bn-jwcc-contracts/)** under the next Joint Warfighting Cloud Capability vehicle. The scale reinforces defense as a durable hyperscale-compute buyer and gives incumbents an October 6 bid deadline.

## Robotics
- **[Kinetix AI raised more than RMB500M ($74.5M) in Angel+ financing](https://autonews.gasgoo.com/articles/news/seeds-former-huawei-executive-develops-humanoid-robot-secures-500-million-yuan-financing-2098403770760597505)** from Vertex Ventures China and others. The unusually large early round funds tighter data-model-hardware iteration and commercialization of embodied-AI robots.
- **[FANUC America and Palladyne AI launched a collaboration to integrate Palladyne IQ across industrial robots](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260908205830/en/Palladyne-AI-and-FANUC-America-Announce-Strategic-Collaboration-to-Advance-Intelligent-Robotic-Automation)**. Adaptive physical-AI software could lower programming and changeover costs—the key adoption barrier in variable manufacturing and logistics tasks.

## Energy
- **[Enbridge agreed to acquire Tallgrass’s crude-transportation business for $2.55B cash](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-09/canada-s-enbridge-to-buy-tallgrass-oil-business-for-2-6-billion)**. Pony Express pipeline and terminal assets deepen Enbridge’s Rockies-to-Cushing network, continuing consolidation around hard-to-replicate midstream infrastructure.
- **[Bannerman completed an A$124M ($89M) placement that fully funds Etango through construction and ramp-up](https://investors.bannermanenergy.com/announcements/7728189)**. The financing moves a major Namibian uranium project toward a fourth-quarter investment decision as nuclear fuel security tightens.
- **[Saudi Arabia shut its East-West oil pipeline after an aerial attack](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/saudis-shut-down-oil-pipeline-houthis-tighten-grip-red-sea-shipping-2026-09-12/)** as Houthi advances also threatened Red Sea access. Disruption to a 4–5 million-bpd bypass route sharply raises geopolitical risk to Gulf export redundancy.

## Crypto
- **[A revised 630-page CLARITY Act draft tightened rules for “controlled” DeFi protocols](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/crypto/articles/630-page-clarity-act-draft-130215803.html)** ahead of a September 15 Senate procedural vote. The text narrows DeFi protections and could materially redraw the SEC/CFTC boundary for on-chain markets.
- **[Nasdaq Ventures agreed to invest $100M in Kraken parent Payward](https://www.msn.com/en-us/news/other/nasdaq-invests-100m-in-kraken-parent-for-tokenized-stocks/gm-GMC0B97FF8)** at a reported $21B valuation. A major exchange operator backing tokenized-equity infrastructure is a strong bridge between regulated market plumbing and always-on crypto rails.

## Emerging Tech
- **[GlobalFoundries finalized a $375M federal award for Quantum Technology Solutions](https://gf.com/news-and-events/news/globalfoundries-and-us-department-of-commerce-finalize-375m-r-and-d-award-to-advance-american-quantum-leadership/)**. The program industrializes QPUs, cryogenic controls, packaging, and superconducting interconnects—moving quantum’s bottleneck from lab science toward domestic manufacturing.
- **[Firefly Aerospace signed SSC Space for two full-capacity Alpha launches from Sweden](https://fireflyspace.com/news/firefly-aerospace-signs-contract-with-ssc-space-for-two-alpha-launches-from-esrange-space-center/)** beginning no earlier than 2028. The deal gives Europe another commercial orbital route for national-security and commercial payloads.
- **[Yarrow Bioscience priced a $150M public offering](https://finance.yahoo.com/healthcare/articles/yarrow-bioscience-announces-pricing-150-011100734.html)** to advance therapies for autoimmune thyroid disease. The raise shows public markets remain open to focused clinical-stage platforms despite biotech’s selective funding environment.
