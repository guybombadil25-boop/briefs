# Investment Brief — 2026-09-16

## AI Infrastructure
- **[Delos Data raised more than $100M](https://www.reuters.com/business/delos-data-chip-startup-founded-by-intel-veterans-raises-100-million-ai-networks-2026-09-15/)** to build interface silicon, servers, and software that keep heterogeneous AI clusters operating through failures. The round targets networking and resiliency—the bottlenecks increasingly leaving expensive accelerators idle.
- **[Euclyd raised over €200M in a Samsung-backed Series A](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-15/chip-startup-euclyd-backed-by-ex-asml-ceo-raises-more-than-200-million)** for inference-focused chips and rack-scale systems, with former ASML CEO Peter Wennink joining as chair. The unusually large pre-deployment bet shows capital is still chasing alternatives to Nvidia on power and inference cost.

## Robotics
- **[Agility Robotics unveiled Digit 5](https://www.agilityrobotics.com/content/agility-unveils-digit-5-humanoid-robot-built-for-cooperatively-safe-work-at-scale)** with redesigned legs, upgraded batteries, higher payload, and safety architecture for working near people without barriers. Built on 65,000 operating hours, it shifts the humanoid race from demos toward certifiable, facility-wide workflows.
- **[Waymo, Nihon Kotsu, and GO agreed to prepare a fully driverless Tokyo service for 2027](https://waymo.com/blog/2026/09/opening-tokyo-in-2027-with-nihon-kotsu-go/)**. A commercial launch in Japan would validate Waymo’s ability to export its stack through local fleet and distribution partners rather than build every market itself.

## Energy
- **[Generate Capital closed a $117M MUFG debt facility](https://generatecapital.com/generate-capital-closes-117-million-community-solar-financing-with-mufg/)** for 18 community-solar projects totaling 114 MWdc in Illinois and New York. Institutional debt availability for distributed generation supports continued deployment despite tighter project-finance conditions.
- **[Nusano was selected for the DOE-backed NRIC Launch Pad program](https://www.ans.org/news/2026-09-15/article-8392/nusano-releases-launch-pad-enrichment-project-details/)** to develop a compact, fluorine-free HALEU production line targeting 5.9 metric tons annually per unit. The process could attack the advanced-reactor fuel bottleneck, though its first unit is not targeted until 2031.

## Crypto
- **[The Senate failed to advance the CLARITY Act](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/15/senate-cloture-vote-on-clarity-act-fails-dealing-regulatory-setback-to-crypto-industry.html)** after cloture fell short of the required 60 votes. Comprehensive U.S. market-structure legislation is now unlikely before the midterms, prolonging exchange, token-classification, and DeFi uncertainty.
- **[The Bank of Russia’s 2027–2029 market-development draft](https://cbr.ru/eng/press/event/?id=32837)** committed to digital payment infrastructure while treating cryptocurrencies and stablecoins used as money substitutes as systemic risks. That split stance favors state-supervised rails over permissionless dollar-linked alternatives in a major sanctions-affected market.

## Emerging Tech
- **[The FDA approved Scholar Rock’s Isembyld](https://www.reuters.com/business/healthcare-pharmaceuticals/us-fda-approves-scholar-rocks-muscle-weakness-drug-2026-09-11/)**, the first muscle-targeted add-on therapy for spinal muscular atrophy. The approval validates myostatin inhibition as a commercial modality alongside gene-directed treatments.
- **[The U.S. publicly acknowledged deploying “on-orbit space control weapons”](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/15/weapons-in-space-us-air-force-meink.html)** for the first time. The disclosure hardens space as a contested defense domain and should accelerate spending on resilient satellites, tracking, servicing, and counterspace systems.
